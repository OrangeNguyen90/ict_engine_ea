## Data contract and invariants

Input: `symbol`, broker symbol properties, UTC and broker timestamps, closed OHLCV bars for configured timeframes, bid/ask quote with age, optional comparison symbols, account reference capital, protection heartbeat and lock state. Each bar carries source, timeframe, UTC open/close and broker timestamp. Reject duplicate, out-of-order, non-positive, or internally inconsistent OHLC bars. Report insufficient history explicitly. Never confirm with bar 0 or with a pivot whose right-side bars were unavailable at the decision timestamp. Persist an `as_of` timestamp and data revision ID with every event. A broker history revision may update the *current recomputation*; preserve prior published event records with a revision annotation instead of pretending they never existed.

All prices and distances use broker tick size and symbol specification. Cross-symbol comparisons use corresponding timestamps and direction of movement, never raw price magnitudes. Convert strategy session times from `America/New_York` via a versioned DST calendar to UTC and then to broker time; missing or ambiguous broker offset yields `UNCERTAIN`.

## Detection rules — proposed by this project, subject to validation

| Concept and role | Detection, confirmation, invalidation | Parameters and false-detection risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Swing and structure: locate internal/external levels | A pivot high is higher than `L` closed highs before and `R` closed highs after; low is symmetric. Confirm at close of rightmost bar, storing pivot time and confirmation time. HH/HL or LH/LL requires two confirmed pivots of each type. BOS is a closed break by at least `break_ticks`; MSS is an opposite closed break of the last protected pivot following a qualified sweep. | `L`, `R`, minimum separation, break ticks, maximum age. Equal highs and noisy pivots can change interpretation; no retrospective signal at pivot time. |
| BSL/SSL and DOL: candidate targets | Candidate levels are prior completed day/week/month highs/lows, confirmed swings, equal highs/lows within tick or ATR tolerance, and completed session highs/lows. Mark untouched, touched, penetrated, reclaimed, or accepted beyond. Rank by direction compatibility, age, distance normalized by ATR, HTF context, and invalidating evidence. | Session calendar, tolerance, ATR window, lookback. A ranked DOL is a hypothesis, not a forecast. Broker D1 boundaries may differ from New York days. |
| Sweep: potential failed penetration | Bullish example: a closed bar trades below an eligible SSL level and closes back above it; bearish mirrors above BSL. Record penetration depth and subsequent MSS separately. A touch or close continuing beyond the level is a different event. | Penetration ticks, reclaim requirement, max bars to MSS. Thin liquidity and spread can produce apparent sweeps. |
| Displacement: directional expansion | Closed candle body/ATR and body/range exceed configured minima, with optional sequence, structure break, or FVG evidence. Mark `candidate` until all required bars close. | ATR period, body multiple, body fraction, sequence length. News spikes and illiquid candles can mimic it. |
| FVG and CE: possible imbalance zone | Three consecutive closed candles C1–C3: bullish `low(C3)>high(C1)`, bearish `high(C3)<low(C1)`. Zone bounds are those two prices; CE is midpoint. Track partial/full mitigation by later closed bars and inversion only after an explicit closed break through the far boundary. | Minimum gap ticks/ATR, age, mitigation definition. Quote gaps and sparse sessions can create mechanical FVGs with weak context. |
| OB and other PD arrays: candidate reaction areas | Candidate final opposing candle or cluster before qualified displacement; require linked break, known liquidity context and bounded search. Store wick/body/hybrid bounds, mitigation, expiry, invalidation and evidence IDs. The provisional chart-only RB/Breaker rules below are not decision-engine confirmations. | Search bars, displacement threshold, bounds, mitigation rule. Calling every prior opposing candle an OB inflates false positives. |
| HTF bias and premium/discount: context | Use confirmed monthly/weekly/daily/H4/H1 pivots and a named dealing range. Equilibrium is the range midpoint. Report conflicting frames as `NEUTRAL` or `UNCERTAIN`; keep supporting levels. | Frame weights, range selection, staleness. Chosen range can dominate conclusions. |
| SMT: intermarket non-confirmation | Pair time-aligned confirmed pivots for selected symbols. For positive relationship, one new high/low without a corresponding new high/low on the other is a candidate; invert comparison logic for a validated negative relationship. Confirm only with adequate bar overlap and configured/observed relationship. | Pair mapping, rolling correlation window/minimum magnitude, max time gap. Correlation regime changes and missing sessions can create false SMT. SMT is confluence only. |
| Sessions and AMD: time context | Label completed Asia/London/New York windows via New York DST mapping. Compute daily/weekly/midnight opens and previous completed ranges. AMD is a descriptive sequence of accumulation range, excursion/reclaim, and expansion; do not infer it from session time alone. | Session windows, timezone database, range width and expansion. Holidays, futures breaks and server D1 candles differ. |

Bullish and bearish examples are symmetric: a sell-side sweep, upward displacement, bullish MSS, bullish FVG retest and BSL target versus a buy-side sweep, downward displacement, bearish MSS, bearish FVG retest and SSL target. These are candidate narratives, not verified market laws.

## Provisional chart-only RB and Breaker rules

Status: project-defined, configurable research heuristics derived from the task specification; not verified ICT quotations, not a statistical edge, and not wired into BUY/SELL approval. Existing OB detection and decision/risk gates are unchanged. Implementation is in `Include/ICTRejectionBreakerDetector.mqh` and `Include/ICTUnifiedRenderer.mqh`.

| Type | Candidate and confirmation | Lifecycle / chart meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rejection Block | A two-left/two-right closed-bar local swing; wick / candle range at least `RB_MinWickRangeRatio` (default 0.50). Within `BlockSignal_ConfirmBars` after pivot confirmation, require a same-direction candle with body/range at least `BlockSignal_MinBodyRatio` (0.60), range / prior-ten-candle mean range at least `BlockSignal_MinRangeAverage` (1.20), and close beyond the prior-ten-candle opposite extreme. Zone is the rejection wick through the candle body edge; midpoint is their mean. A sweep is recorded as supporting evidence, not a mandatory gate. | Confirmation timestamp is the displacement candle close, distinct from pivot open time. Later closed bars mark `RETESTED` on zone overlap or `INVALIDATED` on close beyond the far edge. The chart draws the latest qualifying result per available tier; it does not create an entry. |
| Breaker Block | Reuse the existing OB detector to obtain the latest confirmed opposite OB. Require a later close through the OB's far boundary, then a later local ten-bar liquidity sweep and a directional displacement candle that closes beyond the prior-ten-bar structural extreme. Require the displacement/MSS within the configured confirmation window. An OB failure without the later sweep and MSS is rejected. | Source OB time is retained as formation time; confirmation is the MSS candle close. Later closed bars mark retest or close-based invalidation. The displayed `BB BUY` / `BB SELL` is a chart annotation only; no retest entry, signal score, SL/TP or risk plan is produced by this detector. |

The displacement size filter uses the mean range of the preceding ten closed candles as a lightweight local-volatility proxy, not ATR(14). This is an explicit implementation deviation from the prompt's ATR example and needs comparative testing before any trading use. Inputs are in the `ICT Rejection / Breaker chart signals` group. Data is clipped to snapshot `asof`; results are cached by symbol, timeframe and `asof` and retried when history is unavailable. Invalidated zones are hidden by default. Overlapping zones remain subject to the existing chart timeframe/overlap reservation policy.

Known limitations: only the latest detected opposite OB is considered for a breaker; RB and Breaker are not persisted across restarts; state transitions are recomputed from the bounded history window rather than journaled as immutable events; MTF alignment is visual context, not a detector gate. Native MQL compilation passed, but the isolated MT5 runner did not produce an assertion result in this session. No runtime, demo, Strategy Tester, out-of-sample, or profitability claim is made.

## Decision engine

Event sequence must satisfy `HTF context → eligible liquidity level → sweep → displacement/MSS → active FVG or qualified PD array → retest → valid target → independent risk gates`. Each event has symbol, timeframe, event and confirmation times, price bounds, source bar IDs, parameter version, and expiry. An event may advance only if its required predecessor was already confirmed. Setup invalidates on broken stop premise, expired age, revised source evidence, or opposing structure. States: `NO TRADE`, `WATCH`, `SETUP FORMING`, `WAIT FOR CONFIRMATION`, `ENTRY READY`, `ENTRY INVALIDATED`, `TRADE ACTIVE`, `RISK BLOCKED`. `TRADE ACTIVE` requires an actual matching position, never inference from a signal. `ENTRY READY` requires every mandatory event and the independent risk adapter returning valid.

Output must include bias and competing evidence, DOL and its status, sweep, displacement, MSS, entry zone, stop premise, target level, estimated RR and cash risk, blockers, score components, source timestamps, and model version. A missing item is `unknown`, never silently false or invented.

## Scoring and vetoes

Provisional score weights from the attachment: HTF/DOL 20, sweep 15, structure/MSS 15, displacement 10, FVG/OB 15, premium/discount 10, SMT 5, session 5, entry/execution 5. Sum 100. Missing optional evidence earns zero or `not evaluated` with denominator disclosed. The score cannot override missing mandatory events, stale data, spread, RR < 2, invalid stop/volume/margin, expired setup, or protection lock. It is descriptive and must not be labeled a win probability. Calibrate only on out-of-sample outcomes, with score buckets and sample sizes.
